# Capture and Resume Decision Discussions

Use this branch for design discussions and later tasks that resume their topics. Project creation criteria, format, lifecycle, and task authorization take precedence.

## Use the established topic context

Use the records found through [the skill's discovery step](../SKILL.md#locate-rules-and-records). Follow relevant replacement links and identify the current conclusion, rejected candidates and reasons, open questions, and implementation evidence. Use archives only when historical evidence is needed. Refresh discovery when the scope or relevant records change.

When conditions justify reopening a choice, state what changed and which earlier reasons still hold. Existing proposals are context, not permission to execute pending work.

## Capture meaningful progress

Apply the skill's durability threshold, then update the existing topic owner when a meaningful constraint settles, an evaluated candidate is rejected, or a substantial proposal needs further work. Keep candidates and their outcomes in that topic. A separate rejected record needs independent lasting value.

Preserve the following information where the project template permits it:

- The problem, scope, and constraints that make the choice necessary.
- Selected conclusions and who selected them, including agent choices within existing authorization.
- Agent recommendations that have not become selected choices.
- Evaluated candidates, their current outcomes, and actual rejection reasons.
- Open questions, missing evidence, and the next investigation or decision needed to resume.
- Implementation scope and evidence when work is partly or fully complete.

Write the current synthesis rather than a transcript or a sequence of interview answers. Pending alternatives need no invented rejection reason. Preserve unknowns when evidence is missing.

For the default four-section format, put the current proposal or choice and open questions under `Decision`, candidate outcomes under `Alternatives`, and evidence, uncertainty, and remaining verification under `Consequences and risks`. Additional headings are useful only when the content needs them. A valid shorter project template remains valid.

## Select the state and checkpoint

Use the project's lifecycle or read the [default states](LIFECYCLE.md#default-states). Distinguish evaluation from selection and implementation. A topic can contain settled constraints and rejected candidates while its overall choice remains proposed.

Before pausing or closing the discussion, check that the meaningful progress already reached is persisted and the next agent can tell what remains open. A discussion can end with a proposal. After implementation, replace plans with delivered facts and supporting evidence while preserving why the choice holds.

Discussion skills may guide the interview or write the first draft. Coordinate one topic owner under project rules and maintain that draft instead of creating a parallel record. No external discussion skill is required for capture or resumption.
