# Capture and resume decision discussions

## Resume from the topic

Use records found through [discovery](../SKILL.md#locate-rules-and-records), follow replacement links, and recover the current conclusion, rejected candidates and reasons, open questions, and implementation evidence. Refresh discovery when new information could reveal another relevant record. Consult archives for needed historical evidence.

When reopening a choice, state changed conditions and which earlier reasons still hold. Pending proposals provide context, not execution permission.

## Capture meaningful progress

Apply the skill's durability threshold and update one topic owner as constraints settle, candidates are evaluated, or a substantial proposal develops. Preserve:

- Problem, scope, and meaningful constraints.
- Selected conclusions and who selected them, including authorized agent choices.
- Unselected recommendations, evaluated candidates, their outcomes, and actual reasons.
- Open questions, missing evidence, and the next investigation.
- Implemented scope and evidence, where applicable.

Write a current synthesis. Keep pending candidates and unknowns explicit. A separate rejected record needs independent lasting value. Discussion skills may guide the interview or draft; maintain that same topic under project rules.

## Checkpoint before pausing

Use the project lifecycle or [default states](LIFECYCLE.md#default-states). Distinguish evaluation, selection, and implementation. A topic may contain settled constraints and rejected candidates while its overall choice remains proposed.

Before pausing or closing, confirm that meaningful progress is saved and the next agent can identify what remains open. After implementation, replace plans with delivered facts and evidence while preserving the reasons. A discussion can end with an unresolved proposal.
