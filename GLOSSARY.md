# Decision Recording

Vocabulary used by this collection's decision-recording design.

## Language

**Decision topic**:
A question requiring a durable engineering choice. Its context includes the evaluated candidates, their outcomes, and any unresolved issues.

**Decision record**:
The maintained document for a decision topic, including its reasons and current conclusion or proposal. A record may exist before a choice has been selected or implemented.

**Accepted decision**:
A choice explicitly selected by the user or by an agent within existing task authorization, whose implementation is not yet complete. Acceptance alone does not grant permission to execute the choice.

**Implemented decision**:
A choice whose implementation is complete and supported by evidence. Selection of an approach alone does not establish implementation.
